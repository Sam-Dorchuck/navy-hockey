John J. McMullen Hockey Arena

Every Navy Hockey practice and home game happens here.

The arena

An Olympic-size sheet opened in 2007, named for Dr. John J. McMullen of the legendary Class of 1940. He took a master's degree from MIT and a doctorate from ETH Zurich, founded a naval architecture firm, and later owned the Houston Astros and the New Jersey Devils, who won the Stanley Cup twice under him.
