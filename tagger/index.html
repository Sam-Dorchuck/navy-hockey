How tagging works

  • At the rink: open this page on your phone, start the game, and tap things as they happen. Every tag is time-stamped.
  • From the YouTube stream: paste the link when you create the game (or later in Settings). The video plays here; play it at 1.5–2× and tag.
  • Both: tag live, then open the same game with the video. Line up one faceoff and every live tag snaps onto the video, so you can fix and fill in.
  • Tap the line buttons (F1–F4, D1–D3) the moment a new line hits the ice. That's how ice time and +/- get counted.
