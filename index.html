John J. McMullen Hockey Arena
Brigade Sports Complex · 64 Greenbury Point Road, Annapolis, MD 21402

An Olympic-size sheet opened in 2007, named for Dr. John J. McMullen of the legendary Class of 1940. He took a master's degree from MIT and a doctorate from ETH Zurich, founded a naval architecture firm, and later owned the Houston Astros and the New Jersey Devils, who won the Stanley Cup twice under him. Every practice and home game happens here.

Future Mids

If you're a high school or juniors player looking at the Naval Academy, start here.

Navy Hockey is a top-tier club sport: the intercollegiate schedule and weekly practice routine are on par with varsity programs at the Naval Academy. Players are not recruited the way varsity and NCAA athletes are — admission comes first, through the regular process — but the coaching staff can help you navigate it.

Fill out the player questionnaire
  1. Complete the questionnaireThis is how we start tracking you. If you filled it out and haven't heard back from a team representative, email the Officer Representative directly.
  2. Send your game filmRecent video or links go to Capt Dorchuck at dorchuck@usna.edu.
  3. Apply to the Naval AcademyWork the Steps for Admission and pursue a nomination. Rising seniors should also look at Summer Seminar.
  4. Come see a gamePractices and home games are at McMullen Ice Arena in the Brigade Sports Complex. Tell us you're coming and we'll connect you with players.
